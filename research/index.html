Research

I study grounded multimodal reasoning and reliable, efficient AI. My work also explores agent evaluation and algorithm selection under limited budgets and distribution shift.

Grounded multimodal learning and reasoning

StateDelta

In preparation Planned: ACL Rolling Review, January 2027 cycle, targeting ACL 2027

My undergraduate Signature Work at Duke Kunshan University explores grounded instruction generation from procedural image sequences. I am developing an assembly dataset and a retrieval-augmented vision-language framework that connect instructions to specific components, attachment locations, and supported operations.

Reliable and efficient vision-language inference

ViRel-Budget

Workshop paper GreenMM workshop at ACM Multimedia 2026

I developed a reliability-constrained pipeline that chooses a visual-token pruning budget or a dense fallback before a single VLM call, without fine-tuning the backbone. The work evaluates energy savings alongside a calibrated unsafe-acceptance limit.

Reliable evaluation of agentic AI systems

StableEval Arena

Workshop paper KDD 2026 Workshop on Evaluation and Trustworthiness of Agentic AI

I built an environment-grounded historical-replay benchmark for agentic stablecoin risk assessment. It combines leakage-safe evidence windows and hidden future horizons with structured predictions, deterministic labels, calibration, and reproducible cost and latency logging.

Molecular-Docking Algorithm Selection

Workshop paper NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on AI for Drug Discovery: Bridging the Translation Gap

As a fully funded DKU Summer Research Scholar, I built a retrospective active-learning pipeline for molecular-docking algorithm selection. The work studies how partial acquisition can produce useful selector-training data when docking runtimes differ.

In preparation Planned: AAAI 2027 Workshop on Foundation Models and Generative AI for Finance (FinFM)

As a fully funded DKU–SUFE Summer Research Scholar, I developed a portfolio algorithm-selection pipeline comparing LLM-generated features, pretrained time-series embeddings, and portfolio-trained Transformer representations under distribution shift.
