Grounded multimodal learning and reasoning

StateDelta: State-Transition Grounded Instruction Generation from Procedural Image Sequences

My undergraduate Signature Work at Duke Kunshan University explores grounded instruction generation from procedural image sequences. I am developing an assembly dataset and a retrieval-augmented vision-language framework that connect instructions to specific components, attachment locations, and supported operations.

Dates
June 2026–Present
Advisor
Kaizhu Huang
Status
Manuscript in preparation
Venue / submission plan
Planned: ACL Rolling Review, January 2027 cycle, targeting ACL 2027

Research question

How can structured visual evidence and retrieval support instruction generation across furniture designs and categories while grounding each instruction in the assembly evidence?

My contributions

  • Developing a grounded assembly dataset that links manual diagrams to detailed instructions, component annotations, and source evidence through model-assisted labeling and comprehensive human review.
  • Designing a pipeline from structured visual evidence through semantic and part-based retrieval to image-conditioned instruction generation.
  • Preparing reusable supervision for retrieval evaluation and VLM fine-tuning, with subsequent fine-tuning and RAG integration aimed at transferring knowledge from the curated assembly corpus.

Scope & current status

  • Dataset development and framework design are ongoing. VLM fine-tuning, RAG integration, and cross-design transfer are intended stages of the work.
  • The manuscript is in preparation for a planned January 2027 ACL Rolling Review submission.
