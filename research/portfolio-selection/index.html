Generalizable algorithm selection under distribution shift

Generative-AI-Assisted Portfolio Algorithm Selection under Asset-Family Shift

As a fully funded DKU–SUFE Summer Research Scholar, I developed a portfolio algorithm-selection pipeline comparing LLM-generated features, pretrained time-series embeddings, and portfolio-trained Transformer representations under distribution shift.

Dates
July 2025–March 2026
Advisor
Mustafa Mısır
Status
Manuscript in preparation
Venue / submission plan
Planned: AAAI 2027 Workshop on Foundation Models and Generative AI for Finance (FinFM)

Research question

Which representations help portfolio algorithm selectors transfer across asset families and forward in time?

My contributions

  • Evaluated executable LLM-generated features, pretrained time-series embeddings, and portfolio-trained Transformer representations with random-forest and multilayer-perceptron selectors.
  • Built selectors to choose among classical and learned portfolio optimizers.
  • Investigated representation transfer through centered kernel alignment (CKA), controlled fine-tuning, family-held-out evaluation, and forward-time evaluation.

Findings

  • In the 2025 forward test, portfolio-trained features increased mean annualized return by 12.4 percentage points over a market-feature random-forest selector.

Scope & current status

  • The 12.4-percentage-point improvement is relative to the market-feature random-forest selector in the 2025 forward test.
  • The manuscript is in preparation, with submission to the AAAI 2027 FinFM workshop planned.

Authors & manuscript
