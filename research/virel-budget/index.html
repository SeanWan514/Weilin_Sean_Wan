Reliable and efficient vision-language inference

ViRel-Budget: Reliability-Constrained Visual-Token Budgeting for Green Vision-Language Inference

I developed a reliability-constrained pipeline that chooses a visual-token pruning budget or a dense fallback before a single VLM call, without fine-tuning the backbone. The work evaluates energy savings alongside a calibrated unsafe-acceptance limit.

Research question

Can a VLM use fewer visual tokens while preserving dense-model response behavior under a calibrated reliability constraint?

My contributions

  • Constructed safety labels measuring preservation of dense-model responses on original images and eligible visual interventions.
  • Trained budget-specific risk predictors using pre-inference features and calibrated a policy that selects a pruning budget or dense fallback for one VLM call.
  • Benchmarked on POPE, MMStar, and VisualCounterFact using group-isolated prospective evaluation, independent replication draws, and paired GPU telemetry.
  • First-authored the paper accepted for publication at the ACM Multimedia 2026 GreenMM workshop.

Findings

  • The SCOPE-based controller achieved replicated energy savings of 11.87% for LLaVA-1.5-7B and 10.62% for LLaVA-1.5-13B relative to matched dense inference, while meeting the calibrated unsafe-acceptance limit.

Scope & current status

  • The reported savings refer to the SCOPE-based controller, matched dense LLaVA-1.5-7B and -13B inference, and the evaluated benchmark and replication settings.
  • Reliability is defined through preservation of dense-model responses on original images and eligible visual interventions.
