Reliable evaluation of agentic AI systems

StableEval Arena: A Cost-Aware Agentic Benchmark for Stablecoin Price Stability Prediction

I built an environment-grounded historical-replay benchmark for agentic stablecoin risk assessment. It combines leakage-safe evidence windows and hidden future horizons with structured predictions, deterministic labels, calibration, and reproducible cost and latency logging.

Research question

How can we evaluate agentic stablecoin risk assessment while accounting for evidence leakage, calibration, rare-event failures, and computational cost?

My contributions

  • Built the historical-replay benchmark with leakage-safe evidence windows, hidden future horizons, structured predictions, and deterministic labeling.
  • Integrated calibration and reproducible cost and latency logging into the evaluation.
  • Benchmarked six agents against always-stable, historical-persistence, threshold-rule, and logistic-regression baselines in stress-enriched and natural-distribution settings.
  • First-authored the paper accepted with a selected oral presentation at the KDD 2026 Workshop on Evaluation and Trustworthiness of Agentic AI.

Findings

  • Calibrated agents surpassed historical persistence on aggregate performance in the stress-enriched evaluation.
  • The evaluation revealed substantial rare-event failures that accuracy alone overlooked.

Scope & current status

  • The reported aggregate improvement over historical persistence applies to the enriched evaluation; it is not stated as an improvement across every evaluation setting.
  • Substantial rare-event failures remained despite the aggregate performance gains.
